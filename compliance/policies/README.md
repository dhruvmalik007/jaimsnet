## Policy Framework

All policies will follow the structure:
- **Purpose** — Why this policy exists
- **Scope** — Who and what it applies to
- **Policy Statements** — The rules
- **Responsibilities** — Who enforces what
- **Exceptions** — How to request exceptions
- **Review Cycle** — Annual or on material change
- **Compliance References** — Which frameworks this policy satisfies
